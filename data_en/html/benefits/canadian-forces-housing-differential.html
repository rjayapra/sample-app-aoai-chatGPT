Canadian Forces Housing Differential

Will your CFHD monthly payment (effective July 1 2023) be less than your monthly PLD payment amount as of May 30 2023? If so, refer to the Provisional Post-Living Differential, a temporary policy that will assist you in the transition from PLD to CFHD. 

Additional Resources: CANFORGEN 120/23, PPLD FAQs.

Note: If your circumstances meets the criteria for PPLD, you do not have to actively apply, as your monthly payments will be adjusted automatically.  PPLD does not apply to CAF members whose monthly PLD payments (as of May 20 2023) are lesser in value than their upcoming CFHD payments (effective July 1 2023), nor to CAF members who do not currently receive any PLD.

The Canadian Forces Housing Differential (CFHD) policy is a sophisticated approach (inclusive of GBA+ analysis) with the goal of assisting CAF members in adjusting to housing costs in different locations within Canada.

CFHD rates are updated annually in order to ensure that they remain up to date. This means that CAF members who are eligible for CFHD and who apply can find suitable housing, no matter where they are posted. Thus, providing military members (when applicable) an equitable differential for housing costs when posted to a place of duty.

The information contained within this webpage are to help military members adequately prepare for implementation prior to posting season.
